# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS v4 project. Mongolian-language (`lang="mn"`, Cyrillic) marketing site.

## Users

Owners and staff of small and medium Mongolian businesses (shops, multi-branch stores) who run sales, stock and debts on paper notebooks or ad-hoc tools and are looking at a phone app to replace them.

## Product Purpose

"Цахим дэвтэр" (E-Notebook) is a mobile app (Android, iOS) for sales, inventory, receivables/payables, expenses, reports and multi-branch control. This site is its introduction page. Success: a shop owner understands the app replaces the paper notebook and downloads it.

## Positioning

A digital version of the shopkeeper's paper notebook (дэвтэр) — the ledger Mongolian small traders already keep — rather than a heavy ERP/POS.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive mostly on phones. Content is entirely Mongolian. Primary action: download the app (App Store / Google Play); secondary: contact the company.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Feature list (all must remain on the page): sales recording, inventory, stock management, branch management, customer receivables, supplier payments, income, expenses, sales report, profit report, smart notifications, analytics, stock transfer, owner dashboard, user permissions, multi-branch, payment methods, split payments, returns, and Delivery (Хүргэлт — online sales are recorded into the Delivery menu).
All current menus, section texts and copy are preserved verbatim unless the user says otherwise. Section order may change.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Цахим дэвтэр. Company: Цахим дэвтэр софт ХХК.
- Logo: the app launcher icon (`/images/launcher-icon.jpg`; user is moving it to `/images/launcher.jpg`, which must be supplied by the user).
- Green brand color (#169b62) is kept as the brand anchor. The user also said the palette is otherwise open; treat green as the anchor, the rest as free. (Two answers given; flag if wrong.)
- User-requested direction: take the design language of https://nothingonpurpose.com/ (deadpan mock-official documents, certificate/receipt/spec-sheet devices, stat strip, wall of cards, FAQ, big closing CTA) but not 1:1, reinterpreted as notebooks / ledger paper for an e-notebook app.

## Evidence on Hand

No real app screenshots yet — the user will supply them; the existing code-drawn mock screens (`src/components/screens/`) stand in. No testimonials. Use only stats already on the current site; invent no claims.

## Product Principles

1. The notebook is the metaphor: ledger, receipts, tabs, stitched spines — made from real notebook materials.
2. Every existing feature and section text stays; redesign changes form, not facts.
3. Clear in seconds for a shop owner on a phone: what it is, why, download.
4. No invented proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Cyrillic text must render in every typeface used. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Phone-first layouts.
